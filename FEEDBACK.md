# Feedback

## Ideas noticed while building
- Reports: quick range buttons ("This week", "Last 30 days", "This month") could save picking two dates each time.
- Reports: average vitals are not flagged when outside the usual range, unlike the dashboard tiles.
- Phones: with four menu items, the menu wraps onto two lines (Reports drops to its own row).
- Printed report: out-of-range vitals in the entries table are not marked (High)/(Low), so a doctor reading the printout has to spot them by eye.
